BonziWORLD Rules

  • Swearing is allowed. Don’t spam it or use it to harass people.
  • No NSFW content.
    • This includes pornography, sexual content, sexual roleplay, or explicit discussions.
  • No gore or graphic violence.
    • This includes violent imagery, graphic descriptions, or roleplay involving injury or death.
  • No illegal content or promoting illegal activity.
  • No harassment, hate speech, or targeted abuse.
    • This includes discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or nationality.
  • No threats, doxxing, or sharing private personal information.
  • No spam or intentionally disrupting chatrooms.
  • No exploiting, hacking, or attempting to inject malicious code.
    • All input is sanitized. Don’t try anything stupid.

Moderation

Moderators may warn, mute, kick, or temporarily ban users who break rules or ruin the experience for others.

Repeated or severe violations may result in longer bans without warning.
